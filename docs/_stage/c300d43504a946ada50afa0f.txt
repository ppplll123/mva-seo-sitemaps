<h2>Questions people ask</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>Are there deer in New Jersey?</h3></summary>
<p>Yes, in every county. Police logged animal crashes in all 21 counties in 2023 and 2024, from 74 in Hudson to 2,374 in Monmouth.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What month has the most deer accidents?</h3></summary>
<p>November statewide. By county, November was the worst month in 11 counties and October in 9; Hudson's peak was May.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Which NJ town has the most deer crashes in the fall?</h3></summary>
<p>Bridgewater in Somerset, with 117 in October and November of 2023 and 2024. Raritan Township in Hunterdon (115) and Middletown in Monmouth (112) were next.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What time are deer crashes worst?</h3></summary>
<p>In 13 of the 21 counties, 6 pm to 7 pm was the worst October and November hour. All crash types by hour are on <a href="/new-jersey/when-do-most-car-accidents-happen">when most car accidents happen in New Jersey</a>, and county totals on our <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-accident-statistics">NJ car accident statistics</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>

<h2>Crashed on the Turnpike? Get your crash report</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">The State Police take Turnpike crashes.</strong> Use the toll road option on the <a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJ.gov crash report portal</a>: $5, against $13 on other State Police roads. Allow up to 14 business days.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup></p>
<ul>
<li>Search by case number without dashes, or by license number, plate or last name.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup></li>
<li>Your station is in the police agency box: Moorestown in the south, Cranbury in the middle, Newark in the north (<a href="#stations">station table</a>).<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></li>
<li>Fatal crash: the basic report comes through the <a href="https://www.njta.gov/forms-records/opra-form/">Turnpike Authority OPRA form</a><sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup>; the investigation file needs a subpoena or court order.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup></li>
</ul>

<p>This page is crash records, not live news. For closures right now, check 511NJ or the Turnpike Authority's <a href="https://www.njta.gov/travel-resources/travel-alert/">travel alerts</a>.</p>

<section id="faq" class="df-faqs">
<h2>NJ Turnpike accident questions</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>What happened on the New Jersey Turnpike today?</h3></summary>
<p>Check 511NJ for live closures. Recent deaths are on the <a href="https://nj.gov/njsp/info/fatalacc/">NJ State Police fatal crash statistics</a> list.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Was there a deadly accident on the NJ Turnpike yesterday?</h3></summary>
<p>Check that State Police list. If someone died, the basic report comes only through the OPRA form in <a href="#crash-report">the report steps above</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup></p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What time of day do most Turnpike crashes happen?</h3></summary>
<p>4 pm is the worst hour (192 injury crashes in 2023 and 2024), and 3 pm to 6 pm holds 19 percent. Sunday had the most (444). Compare <a href="/new-jersey/when-do-most-car-accidents-happen">when crashes happen across New Jersey</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Is the Turnpike more dangerous than the Parkway?</h3></summary>
<p>It had more deaths in 2023 and 2024, 43 against 40<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup>, on fewer injury crashes, 2,840 against 3,461. Route 1 had 50. See <a href="/new-jersey/garden-state-parkway-accidents">Garden State Parkway crash data</a> and <a href="/new-jersey/route-1-nj-accidents">Route 1 crash data</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup></p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Can I bring a claim against the Turnpike Authority?</h3></summary>
<p>A claim against the Authority itself, not the other driver, needs a written notice within 90 days, a rule the state Supreme Court quoted in a 2019 Turnpike Authority case. The steps are in <a href="/new-jersey/nj-tort-claims-notice-car-accident-public-entity">notice of claim against a public body</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup></p>
</details></section>
<section class="place-common df-rules" id="what-these-numbers-mean">
<details>
<summary>Rules that apply anywhere in New Jersey</summary>
<ul>
<li>Your own auto policy's PIP pays eligible medical expenses "no matter who caused the auto accident," up to your policy's limit and after its deductible and co-pay. If you chose your health insurance as primary, it pays first and PIP pays second. A claim against the other driver for pain and suffering is separate: it depends on fault and on the lawsuit option on your policy.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup></li>
<li>Being partly at fault does not stop you from recovering, as long as your share is not greater than the other driver's, or the combined share of all the drivers you claim against (the "51 percent" rule). Your damages are reduced by your share.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-7">7</a></sup></li>
<li>If someone is hurt or killed, or property damage to any one person is over $500, the driver must tell the police right away and send a written report to the Motor Vehicle Commission within 10 days, unless a police officer files one. If police did not investigate, New Jersey's SR-1 self-reporting form is the form for that.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-8">8</a></sup></li>
</ul>

<details id="method"><summary>How we counted</summary>
<ul>
<li>Records: NJDOT crash records 2023 to 2025 with route number 95 or 700 and a state road name, normalized by nj-crashes, deaths added from State Police fatal statistics. Route 700 is the Turnpike south of its I-95 section; the I-95 stretch in Fort Lee is included.</li>
<li>2023 and 2024 are complete; 2025 is preliminary. Headline counts use 2023 and 2024.</li>
<li>Mileposts and stations use 2024 and 2025. Milepost 77 means 77.0 to just under 78, on mainline records only.</li>
<li>Station coding is nearly complete only from 2024: in Elizabeth, 683 of 696 Turnpike records carry a station in 2024, but 145 of 649 in 2023. That is why our <a href="/new-jersey/elizabeth-nj-car-accidents">Elizabeth page</a>, counting 2023 and 2024, shows a lower State Police share.</li>
<li>Milepost stretches use 2023 and 2024, with the station share from 2024 records. 612 of 14,261 records with no mainline milepost, or one more than 8 miles from the other records of the same town, are left out.</li>
<li>Exit areas use 2023 and 2024: each record goes to the nearest exit within 1.5 miles of its ramps, by route milepost; 2,384 records farther away count only in the stretch table. Exit mileposts come from the Wikipedia I-95 list, which uses the same NJDOT mileposts as the records: 32 of its 34 exits sit inside the milepost range of their own town's crash records. The Turnpike's own mileage, in its Wikipedia exit list, runs about 45.4 miles higher than the I-95 route mileposts from exit 7 north (20 exits checked, 44.9 to 45.7); 153 records that carried that mileage were converted. Exits 18E and 18W are toll plazas with no interchange milepost, and exit 70 has none listed, so their crashes count at the nearest listed exit.</li>
<li>A filled station is read as a State Police report. These are counts, not risk rates or fault findings.</li>

<section id="hardest">
<h2>What is the hardest injury to prove?</h2>
<p>In our New Jersey record, a disc herniation: 4 of the 6 herniation claims that got a threshold ruling ended with no pain and suffering award.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>The four losses split three ways. Orlando lacked proof of permanence or cause, Williams-Siraj lacked proof the other drivers caused her injuries, and Joo lacked medical evidence the crash worsened an older condition. Zavis lost on a fresh review of the record.</p>
<p>The diagnosis label did not separate winners from losers. Radiculopathy, nerve pain spreading from the spine, appears in both wins and in two losses, Zavis and Williams-Siraj.</p>
<p>Words about an earlier, worsened condition appear in four claims: one won (Presbery), two lost and one went back for a new trial. What counted was medical proof of the change, not the phrase.</p>
</section>
<section id="bulging">
<h2>How much compensation can I expect for a bulging disc after a car accident?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">Our record cannot price a bulge on its own.</strong> Bulges appear in 8 of the 9 disc claims, but every one of those also had a herniation.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>The only bulge case with a dollar figure is Morales-Hurtado, a Bergen jitney rear-end: $50,000 for pain and suffering plus $71,615.73 in past medical bills. The appeals court reversed that verdict, so it is not a benchmark either.</p>
</section>
<section id="whiplash">
<h2>How much do whiplash cases usually settle for?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">None of our coded injury descriptions uses the word whiplash, so our record gives no whiplash figure.</strong> Our coded descriptions list cervical strain or sprain instead.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>Three disc claims list a neck strain or sprain, with three different endings. Presbery kept $240,000, Joo got nothing for pain and suffering, and McKoy won a new trial.</p>
<p>In all three the strain sat beside disc herniations, so the strain alone cannot be priced from these cases. When whiplash pain tends to start is on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-how-long-car-accident-symptoms-can-take-to-show-up">how long car accident symptoms take to show up</a>.</p>
</section>
<section id="threshold">
<h2>Does a herniated disc meet the verbal threshold in NJ?</h2>
<p>It can, with a doctor's permanency certification served within 60 days after the defendant answers the lawsuit.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The proof rules are on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-10-ways-to-prove-pain-and-suffering-after-an-accident">ways to prove pain and suffering in New Jersey</a>, and the two lawsuit options on <a href="/new-jersey/is-nj-a-no-fault-state">is NJ a no-fault state</a>.</p>
<p>Read against the nine cases, a disc claim needs four things in the file before any number means much:</p>
<ol class="df-steps">
<li>The lawsuit option on your declarations page. With no limitation on lawsuit, the permanency test does not apply.</li>
<li>A timely permanency certification resting on imaging or other objective findings.</li>
<li>A medical opinion that this crash caused the disc injury, the gap in Orlando and Williams-Siraj.</li>
<li>If your spine showed wear before, medical proof that the crash made it worse, the gap in Joo.</li>
</ol>
</section>
<section id="who-pays">
<h2>How much does accident insurance pay for a herniated disc?</h2>
<p>Medical bills go first to your own PIP, and DOBI says most drivers choose a $250,000 medical limit.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> How that works is on <a href="/new-jersey/pip-coverage-limits-medical-bills-new-jersey">PIP coverage limits for medical bills</a>.</p>
<p>Zavis and Joo claimed against their own insurers under UM or UIM coverage, and both still failed on permanency. Those disputes are on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-uninsured-underinsured-motorist-appeals-decisions">NJ uninsured and underinsured motorist appeals</a>, and more statewide guides on the <a href="/new-jersey/new-jersey-car-accident-help">New Jersey car accident help hub</a>.</p>

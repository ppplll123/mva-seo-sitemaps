<section id="vehicle-number">
<h2>Is Vehicle 1 the driver who caused the crash?</h2>
<p>No. A vehicle number is an identifier that links each driver, owner and passenger to the diagram and narrative; it is not a fault finding.</p>
<p>Follow your number through every entry; an owner listed apart from the driver is only identified, not blamed. Our guide to <a href="/new-jersey/nj-what-evidence-helps-prove-fault-after-an-nj-crash">what evidence proves fault in NJ</a> covers what weighs beyond the report.</p>

<p>Match the vehicle numbers in Box 144, the diagram, to the account in Box 145, the narrative, then compare both with the Box 105 code.</p>

<figure><a href="https://cdn.dorik.com/6acbe74237318c00111fbd1e/images/njdot-official-annotated-front-AkCXZ.webp" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><img alt="Official NJDOT annotated NJTR-1 front page showing crash location, driver and vehicle details, commercial vehicle details and persons involved." height="1584" loading="lazy" src="https://cdn.dorik.com/6acbe74237318c00111fbd1e/images/njdot-official-annotated-front-AkCXZ.webp" width="1224"/></a><figcaption>Front: location, people and vehicles. NJDOT manual, page 4.</figcaption></figure>

NJDOT manual, page 5.</figcaption></figure>
</div>
<p>Worked example for a rear-end crash (our <a href="/new-jersey/rear-end-crash-new-jersey">rear-end crash guide for New Jersey</a> covers the claim side):</p>
<ol class="df-steps">
<li><strong>Find your vehicle number</strong> from the driver and vehicle details on the front.</li>
<li><strong>Check Box 105.</strong> Code 01 means a same-direction rear-end first event.</li>
<li><strong>Read Box 144.</strong> Follow the same numbers in the drawing and note any attached diagram.</li>
<li><strong>Read Box 145.</strong> Separate the physical facts, what each driver or witness said and the officer's own evaluation.</li>
<li><strong>Write down any mismatch</strong>, such as a vehicle number that does not fit the narrative.</li>
</ol>
<p>The manual's example diagram is marked not to scale, so do not measure speed or distance from it. If vehicles moved before the officer arrived, the drawing may be representative, with the reason given in the narrative.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup></p>

</section>
<section id="asterisk">
<h2>What does an asterisk in the insurance section mean?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">It flags a situation the officer explains in the narrative; on its own it does not prove the other vehicle was uninsured.</strong> The manual lists several uses of the asterisk in insurance information, so the narrative tells you which one applies.</p>
</section>
<section id="errors">
<h2>What if your NJ crash report is wrong or incomplete?</h2>
<p>Ask the agency that wrote it about a correction or supplement, naming the exact box or passage in question.</p>
<p>A mention of photos does not mean they are attached: serious or fatal crash files are a <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-accident-report#state-police">separate records request outside the basic NJTR-1</a>.</p>
<p>Still need the report itself? Our guide on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-accident-report">how to get your New Jersey car accident report</a> lists each agency's route and fee. For how fault percentages change a payout, see <a href="/new-jersey/partly-at-fault-nj-car-accident-real-fault-splits">real NJ fault splits</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup></p>

</section>

<section id="faq" class="df-faqs">
<h2>Quick answers on NJ crash report codes</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>What is a code 4 injury on an NJ crash report?</h3></summary>
<p>Code 04 in Box 86, physical condition, means possible injury. It records the officer's view at the scene, not a diagnosis, so a later medical record can show more.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What are the police codes on an NJ crash report?</h3></summary>
<p>They are numbered NJTR-1 entries that mean something only inside their own box. The same 03 is a right-angle crash in Box 105 and a suspected minor injury in Box 86. See the <a href="#crash-type-codes">crash-type code table</a>.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What is the code for a car accident?</h3></summary>
<p>Box 105 codes the crash type: 01 is a rear-end, 02 a sideswipe, 03 a right angle, 06 a struck parked vehicle and 07 a left turn or U-turn.</p>
</details></section>

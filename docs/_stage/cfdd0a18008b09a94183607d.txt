<h1>Car accident in North Bergen, NJ? Three roads hold more than half of the town's injury accidents</h1>

<section id="short-answer">
<p><strong>If you were hurt in a car accident in North Bergen, your police report most likely comes from the North Bergen Police Department Records Bureau at 4233 Kennedy Boulevard.</strong> Two copies cost $5 in cash in person. Bring the Police Incident (CAD) number and call (201) 392-2145 first to check that the report is ready. If the accident was on the New Jersey Turnpike, the report comes from the State Police portal instead (details <a href="#report">below</a>).</p>
<p class="source">Source: <a href="https://northbergenpolice.com/records-bureau-info/">North Bergen Police Department, Records Bureau Info</a> and <a href="https://northbergenpolice.com/schedule-of-fees/">Schedule of Fees</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10.</p>
<p>In 2023 and 2024, 802 police-reported accidents in North Bergen left someone hurt or killed, about 1.1 a day. 1,096 people were hurt and 5 died. Only Jersey City has more injury accidents in Hudson County, and North Bergen ranks 30th among New Jersey municipalities.</p>
<p>Three roads carry 417 of those 802 accidents, or 52 percent: Tonnelle Avenue (Route 1), Kennedy Boulevard and Route 495, the Lincoln Tunnel approach.</p>
<p class="source">Source: NJDOT crash records 2023 to 2025 as normalized by hudcostreets/nj-crashes, fatal crashes supplemented from the NJ State Police feed; our analysis, files <code>data/profiles/north-bergen-nj-car-accidents.json</code>, <code>data/counties/hudson.json</code> and <code>data/north-bergen-nj-car-accidents/north_bergen_cuts.csv</code>. North Bergen is 12.8 percent of Hudson County's 6,279 injury accidents in 2023 and 2024.</p>

<section id="sideswipes">
<h2>Sideswipes outnumber rear-end accidents here, but rarely hurt anyone</h2>
<p>Police coded 1,202 same-direction sideswipes in North Bergen in 2023 and 2024, more than the 1,103 rear-end accidents. Only 111 sideswipes, about 9 percent, left someone hurt. For rear-end accidents it was 251, about 23 percent.</p>
<p>Most sideswipes happened on the three big roads: 340 on Tonnelle Avenue, 240 on Kennedy Boulevard and 169 on Route 495. Even so, sideswipes are a bigger share of injury accidents here than statewide.</p>

<section id="deaths">
<h2>Six of the nine people killed since 2023 were on foot</h2>
<p>The records list 9 people killed in North Bergen car accidents from 2023 through 2025, in 8 accidents. Six of the nine were pedestrians. 2025 is preliminary, so its count may still rise.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>2023: 5 killed.</strong> Four pedestrians: two on Tonnelle Avenue, one on Bergenline Avenue and one on Route 3. The fifth death was a sideswipe on a local street.</li>
<li><strong>2024: none.</strong> No one was killed in a North Bergen car accident that year.</li>
<li><strong>2025 (preliminary): 4 killed in 3 accidents.</strong> A pedestrian on Tonnelle Avenue, a pedestrian on Bergenline Avenue, and a head-on accident on Tonnelle Avenue that killed two people.</li>
</ul>
<p>Six of the eight fatal accidents happened after dark. Tonnelle Avenue accounts for 5 of the 9 deaths.</p>

<section id="report">
<h2>Getting your North Bergen accident report</h2>
<p>Which office has your report depends on which agency came to the scene. If North Bergen police came, the report is at the department's Records Bureau.</p>
<h3>North Bergen Police Department Records Bureau</h3>
<ul>
<li>Pick up incident and accident reports in person on the first floor of Police Headquarters, 4233 Kennedy Boulevard, North Bergen, NJ 07047.</li>
<li>Hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 3:30pm, except national holidays. Call (201) 392-2145 before you go to make sure the report is available.</li>
<li>Have the Police Incident (CAD) number ready; the department says it speeds up the request. Questions go to bci@northbergenpd.com.</li>
<li>Fees: $5 cash for two copies in person, or $10 by money order for two copies by mail, with a written request and a self-addressed stamped envelope.</li>
</ul>
<p class="source">Source: <a href="https://northbergenpolice.com/records-bureau-info/">NBPD Records Bureau Info</a> and <a href="https://northbergenpolice.com/schedule-of-fees/">NBPD Schedule of Fees</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10.</p>
<h3>Accidents on the New Jersey Turnpike</h3>
<p>North Bergen's stretch of I-95 is the New Jersey Turnpike. State Police and Turnpike Authority crash reports are requested online through the NJSP crash report portal, where a toll road report costs $5.00. The basic report can be downloaded once it is ready and approved. Questions go to NJSPRecords@NJSP.gov or (609) 882-2000 ext. 2234.</p>
<p class="source">Source: <a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports portal</a> and the NJSP Criminal Justice Records Bureau page, retrieved 2026-11-10 (research/nj-primary-sources.md, section 8). TURNPIKE" (<code>data/north-bergen-nj-car-accidents/north_bergen_cuts.csv</code>, cuts road_system_by_route_2023_2024 and road_names_by_route_2023_2024).</p>
<h3>If another agency responded</h3>
<p>If a different police agency wrote the report, ask that agency for it; the report number and agency name are on the paperwork you got at the scene. The NJTR-1 crash report is completed by police only.</p>

<section class="place-common" id="what-these-numbers-mean">
  <h2>What these numbers can and cannot tell you</h2>
  <p>The counts on this page are police-reported car accidents from NJDOT crash records, analyzed by us. They show where and when accidents where someone was hurt happened in North Bergen. They do not show who was at fault in any accident, and they say nothing about whether you have a claim or what it could be worth.</p>
  <h3>Rules that apply anywhere in New Jersey</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Your own auto policy's PIP pays your medical bills "no matter who caused the auto accident." A claim against the other driver for pain and suffering is separate and depends on fault and on the lawsuit option on your policy. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/division_consumers/pdf/autoguide2026.pdf">NJ DOBI Auto Insurance Buyer's Guide 2026</a>, p. 2)</span></li>
    <li>Being partly at fault does not stop you from recovering, as long as your share is not greater than the other driver's (the "51 percent" rule). Your damages are reduced by your share. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:15-5.1; <a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/ins_ombudsman/ombuds_acnsfaq.html">NJ DOBI comparative negligence FAQ</a>)</span></li>
    <li>If someone is hurt, the driver must tell the police right away. If police did not investigate, New Jersey's SR-1 self-reporting form is the form for that. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 39:4-130; <a href="https://dot.nj.gov/transportation/refdata/accident/selfreporting.shtm">NJDOT self-reporting crash form</a>)</span></li>
    <li>An injury lawsuit generally must be filed within two years, and a claim against a public body (a town, the state, NJ Transit) needs a written notice within 90 days. These are general rules with exceptions; check your own dates with a lawyer. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:14-2; N.J.S.A. 59:8-8)</span></li>
  </ul>

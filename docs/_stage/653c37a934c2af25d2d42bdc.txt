<section id="rate">
<h2>What is the car accident rate in New Jersey?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">About 1 crash in 5 hurts someone: 112,966 of 514,320 crashes in 2023 and 2024, or 22 percent.</strong> Roughly 1 in 420 killed someone.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>Each injury crash hurt 1.4 people on average.</p>

<section id="roads">
<h2>Which roads in NJ have the most crashes?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">County roads, as a group: 32 percent of injury crashes in 2023 and 2024.</strong> Town streets had 28 percent and NJDOT state highways 27 percent.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>State highways carried the most deaths, 443 of 1,294, or 34 percent. Toll-road authorities had 7 percent of injury crashes and interstates 5 percent. Road pages break down <a href="/new-jersey/i-295-nj-accidents">I-295 crashes by county</a>, <a href="/new-jersey/i-287-nj-accidents">I-287 accidents by milepost</a>, <a href="/new-jersey/route-4-nj-accidents">Route 4 crash data</a> and <a href="/new-jersey/atlantic-city-expressway-accidents">Atlantic City Expressway accidents</a>.</p>
<p>Named roads are ranked on <a href="/new-jersey/most-dangerous-roads-nj">the most dangerous roads in NJ</a>, corners on <a href="/new-jersey/most-dangerous-intersections-nj">the most dangerous intersections in NJ</a>, and hours on <a href="/new-jersey/when-do-most-car-accidents-happen">when most car accidents happen</a>.</p>
</section>

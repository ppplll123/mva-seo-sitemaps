<section id="which-count">
<h2>Which highway is the most dangerous?</h2>
<p>It depends on the count: toll roads lead on injury crashes, while older surface pikes climb when you count deaths.</p>
<ul>
<li>The Atlantic City Expressway ranks 48th for injury crashes (198) but 16th for deaths (13).</li>
<li>US 322 ranks 32nd for injury crashes and joint 12th for deaths, with 16.</li>
<li>Route 55 ranks 40th for injury crashes and joint 20th for deaths, with 10.</li>
<li>Route 4 ranks 33rd for injury crashes, with 397, and recorded 1 death.</li>
</ul>
<p>Pedestrians drive much of the gap. On the White Horse Pike 18 of 33 people killed were on foot, against 4 of 43 on the Turnpike, counting the pedestrians-killed field of each crash record.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> Our <a href="/new-jersey/pedestrian-crash-new-jersey">pedestrian crash guide</a> covers what follows a crash on foot.</p>

<h1>Washington Township, Gloucester County car accidents: the deaths were on county roads, not Route 42</h1>

<p>Washington Township in Gloucester County had 668 police-reported car accidents where someone was hurt or killed in 2023 and 2024. Those accidents left 929 people hurt and 7 dead. No other Gloucester County town had more: the township holds 17% of the county total and ranks 40th statewide. Route 42 carries the most accidents in the township, yet none of its 893 recorded accidents from 2023 to 2025 killed anyone. Every death in those three years was on a county road, though one preliminary 2025 death was at the County Route 689 crossing of Route 42. Four of the seven people killed in 2023 and 2024 were pedestrians, all after dark. Most records here name no State Police unit, so the report usually comes from the township police.</p>
<p class="source">The 668, 929 and 7 cover the two full years, 2023 and 2024; anything from 2025 is preliminary and marked as such. <section id="pedestrians">
<h2>Four of Gloucester County's 11 pedestrian deaths in 2023 and 2024</h2>
<p>Gloucester County recorded 11 pedestrians killed in 2023 and 2024. Four of them were in Washington Township, twice as many as any other town in the county. Franklin Township had two; no other town had more than one. Three of the four died in the last two months of 2024, two in November and one in December.</p>
<p>That stands out because pedestrian accidents are not common here overall. They were 2.4% of the township's accidents where someone was hurt or killed, against 6.6% statewide. The township had 17 pedestrian accidents in the two years. All four deaths happened in the dark, on county roads.</p>

<section id="police-report">
<h2>Township police, State Police or the Expressway: who has your report</h2>
<p>For most accidents in Washington Township, the report is held by the township police. In 2024 and 2025, 2,428 of the 2,541 recorded accidents carry no State Police station name. Start with the case number the responding officer gave you at the scene. We could not open the township's website to check its records page, so we do not list a link, fee or wait time.</p>
<p>Three roads are different:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Route 55.</strong> All 11 recorded accidents in the township in 2024 and 2025 name the State Police Bellmawr station.</li>
<li><strong>Route 42.</strong> 40 of 543 records name a State Police station, almost all Bellmawr. The rest name none. If a trooper came, use the State Police route below.</li>
<li><strong>Atlantic City Expressway.</strong> All 57 records name a State Police unit, 53 of them the Atlantic City Expressway unit.</li>
</ul>
<p class="source">Source: NJDOT crash records 2024 and 2025 (2025 preliminary) via hudcostreets/nj-crashes; our cuts in <code>wt-station-by-route-2024-2025.csv</code> and <code>wt-station-share-by-route-2024-2025.csv</code>. The station field is undocumented, so treat it as a hint. Which agency responded is shown on your own report, not in this tally.</p>
<p><strong>State Police reports</strong> are requested through the <a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports portal</a>. Once the State Police mark a report "ready and approved," the portal lets you download it. Six years is how long a report stays available there, measured from the year of the crash. Fees listed: $13.00 for a non-toll road report and $5.00 for a toll road report. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports portal</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></p>
<p>Trouble with the online service goes by email to njsprecords@njsp.gov. Records requests for a serious or fatal crash can take up to one month, and the portal asks you to call (609) 882-2000 ext. 2234 about them only after that month has passed. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports portal</a>; <a href="https://njsp.njoag.gov/identification-amp-information-technology-section/criminal-justice-records-bureau/">NJSP Criminal Justice Records Bureau</a>; both retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></p>
<p><strong>Atlantic City Expressway reports</strong> are requested in writing from the Atlantic City Expressway Authority, P.O. Box 389, Hammonton, NJ 08037, (609) 965-7200 ext. 108. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports portal</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></p>
<p><strong>No police at the scene?</strong> Then there is no NJTR-1 to request, since that crash report is a police form. In that case the drivers write up the crash themselves on the SR-1. <span class="source">(<a href="https://dot.nj.gov/transportation/refdata/accident/policeres.shtm">NJDOT police resources</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></p>
<p>When the report arrives, DOBI advises: "Review report for details, such as how many passengers were listed... If you see inconsistencies, let your insurance company know and contact the police department that responded to the scene." <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/division_consumers/pdf/everythingauto2026.pdf">NJ DOBI, Everything You Wanted to Know About Auto Insurance (2026)</a>, p. 25, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></p>

<section class="place-common" id="what-these-numbers-mean">
  <h2>What these numbers can and cannot tell you</h2>
  <p>The counts on this page are police-reported car accidents from NJDOT crash records, analyzed by us. They show where and when accidents where someone was hurt happened in Washington Township, Gloucester County. They do not show who was at fault in any accident, and they say nothing about whether you have a claim or what it could be worth.</p>
  <h3>Rules that apply anywhere in New Jersey</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Your own auto policy's PIP pays your medical bills "no matter who caused the auto accident." A claim against the other driver for pain and suffering is separate and depends on fault and on the lawsuit option on your policy. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/division_consumers/pdf/autoguide2026.pdf">NJ DOBI Auto Insurance Buyer's Guide 2026</a>, p. 2)</span></li>
    <li>Being partly at fault does not stop you from recovering, as long as your share is not greater than the other driver's (the "51 percent" rule). Your damages are reduced by your share. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:15-5.1; <a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/ins_ombudsman/ombuds_acnsfaq.html">NJ DOBI comparative negligence FAQ</a>)</span></li>
    <li>If someone is hurt, the driver must tell the police right away. If police did not investigate, New Jersey's SR-1 self-reporting form is the form for that. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 39:4-130; <a href="https://dot.nj.gov/transportation/refdata/accident/selfreporting.shtm">NJDOT self-reporting crash form</a>)</span></li>
    <li>An injury lawsuit generally must be filed within two years, and a claim against a public body (a town, the state, NJ Transit) needs a written notice within 90 days. These are general rules with exceptions; check your own dates with a lawyer. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:14-2; N.J.S.A. 59:8-8)</span></li>
  </ul>

<section id="method">
<h2>Method and sources for the Washington Township counts</h2>
<p>Data: NJDOT crash records for 2023 (NJDOT per-table archive) and 2024 and 2025 (NJDOT Crash Data Portal), as normalized by the open-source project <a href="https://github.com/hudcostreets/nj-crashes">hudcostreets/nj-crashes</a>, with fatal crashes supplemented from the NJ State Police fatal feed. File downloaded 2026-11-06. The township means NJDOT municipality "Washington Twp" in Gloucester County.</p>
<p>An accident is counted under "hurt or killed" when its police record lists at least one injury or one death. The two full years are 2023 and 2024; every 2025 figure carries a preliminary label. The statewide rank of 40 compares NJDOT county-municipality pairs. A handful of those pairs are miscodes, such as a Gloucester County record coded Perth Amboy, each with one or two records. They sit far below the township, so leaving them out does not change its rank. Road counts combine spellings by NJDOT route number. "County road", "state highway" and "township street" follow NJDOT's road_system code. Intersection rankings use 2024 and 2025, because street naming changed between exports, and they are a floor because spelling variants are not merged. The State Police station field is filled only in some records and is undocumented. Nothing here is divided by traffic volume, so a road with more accidents is not shown to be more dangerous per car.</p>
<p>The township profile comes from <code>lane2-content/data/build_place_profiles.py</code>. The township cuts come from <code>washington_twp_cuts.py</code> and <code>wt_route42_by_year.py</code> in <code>lane2-content/data/washington-township-gloucester-nj-car-accidents/</code>, and each CSV there opens with a header line on period and method. Rules shared by every data page on this site are written up in <code>lane2-content/data/README.md</code>.</p>

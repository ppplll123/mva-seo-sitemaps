<section id="what-is-route-9">
<h2>What is Route 9 in New Jersey?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">It is one route number filed three ways.</strong> The main line runs from Lower Township in Cape May County to Woodbridge, at mileposts from under 1 to about 136.</p>
<p>In Bergen County the records restart as Route 9W, or a "western alignment" on Sylvan, Fletcher and Lemoine Avenues, with mileposts near zero in Fort Lee. The shared Route 1&amp;9 is filed under Route 1, so it is counted on <a href="/new-jersey/route-1-nj-accidents">Route 1 accident data, including Route 1&amp;9</a>.</p>
<p>Your report may not say "Route 9" at all. Police often write the local name instead; our guide on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-how-to-read-a-new-jersey-crash-report-after-an-accident">how to read a New Jersey crash report</a> explains the other boxes.</p>

<section id="answer" class="df-answer">
<p class="df-lead"><strong>PIP pays first.</strong> After a New Jersey car accident, your own auto policy's Personal Injury Protection pays eligible medical bills first, whoever caused the crash. Your health plan pays first only if you chose the health-primary option.</p>
<p>2 of the 14 published PIP appeals rulings in our case file turn on that option, and both left the driver or the providers holding the bill.</p>

<section id="need-pip">
<h2>Do I need personal injury cover on car insurance if I have health insurance?</h2>
<p>Yes. PIP is part of every New Jersey auto policy, Standard and Basic, so having health insurance never removes it. PIP also backs you up if the named plan has lapsed by the crash date, and the auto insurer has no duty to check that it is active.</p>
<p>What a health plan can change is who pays first. On a Standard Policy you may name your health insurer as primary, and PIP then pays second. Medicare and Medicaid are the exception, as the table below shows.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>

</section>
<section id="what-pages-miss">
<h2>What do most guides miss about choosing health insurance as primary?</h2>
<p>Most guides treat health primary as a simple swap of payers, but New Jersey's appeals courts have put the risk on you when the named plan fails.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>James v. State Farm (2019):</strong> after a health-primary election, the auto insurer had no duty to check whether the household's health coverage was real. The court held it immune under a state statute.</li>
<li><strong>Palisades v. Horizon Blue Cross Blue Shield (2021):</strong> a PIP carrier that paid bills the health plan should have paid first had no statutory right to recover from the health insurer. It had to go after the providers or use an assignment.</li>
</ul>
<p>If the bill goes to the wrong insurer, the insurers will not settle it between themselves. Our page on <a href="/new-jersey/nj-pip-appeals-decisions-permanent-injury">PIP appeals decisions on denied medical bills</a> covers the other 12 rulings.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup></p>

</section>
<section id="pip-primary">
<h2>What is PIP primary, and what changes when your health plan pays first?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">PIP primary, the default, means PIP pays first; under health primary, your plan's own rules apply and PIP only fills gaps.</strong> DOBI lists the changes:</p>
<ul>
<li>The plan's deductibles, co-payments and coverage limits apply.</li>
<li>Its pre-certification rules apply, so treatment may need the plan's approval first.</li>
<li>Your auto insurer "will only pay for necessary expenses not covered by your health policy or plan."</li>
</ul>
<p>When PIP is primary, a health plan pays second: the smaller of allowable bills PIP left (its deductible, co-pay, bills over your limit) or what the plan would have paid as primary. Its own deductible and co-pay may reduce that.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup></p>

</section>

<section id="worth-it">
<h2>Is PIP coverage worth it, or should your health plan pay first?</h2>
<p>Health primary only pays off if your plan covers car accident injuries and stays active; DOBI tells drivers to confirm that before choosing it.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>PIP primary:</strong> your auto policy's PIP deductible and co-payment apply, and PIP's treatment review rules decide what gets paid.</li>
<li><strong>Health primary:</strong> the plan's rules, listed above, apply first.</li>
</ul>
<p>The PIP deductible, co-payment and limit options are on our page about <a href="/new-jersey/pip-coverage-limits-medical-bills-new-jersey">PIP coverage limits and medical bills in New Jersey</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup></p>

</section>
<section id="check-your-election">
<h2>How do I check which PIP option I chose?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">Look at the Coverage Selection Form from your auto insurer.</strong> A health-primary choice lists your health plan's name and policy or group number.</p>
<ol class="df-steps">
<li>Find your latest Coverage Selection Form, or ask your auto insurer which PIP option is on the policy.</li>
<li>If health insurance is primary, call the plan. Ask whether it covers car accident injuries and which treatments need approval first.</li>
<li>Give every provider both cards and say which one is primary, so the first bill goes to the right insurer.</li>
<li>Keep every explanation of benefits and denial letter. Our guide to <a href="/new-jersey/medical-records-injury-claim-new-jersey">medical records for an injury claim</a> lists what to collect.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup></li>
</ol>

</section>
<section id="hospital">
<h2>Which insurer gets the hospital bill after a car accident?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">The primary one: PIP, unless your Coverage Selection Form names your health plan.</strong> Under health primary, the hospital bills your plan first and PIP pays necessary expenses the plan leaves unpaid.</p>
<p>How PIP handles the hospital bill itself is in our section on <a href="/new-jersey/pip-coverage-limits-medical-bills-new-jersey#hospital-bill">who pays a hospital bill after a car accident in NJ</a>.</p>
</section>
<section id="denials">
<h2>What if your health plan or PIP denies a bill?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">First check which insurer sent the denial, because under health primary there are two payers and two routes.</strong> Send a health plan denial to your auto insurer, since PIP still pays necessary expenses the plan does not cover. A PIP denial follows the <a href="#pip-arbitration">arbitration route below</a>.</p>
<p>Bills that neither insurer covers, or that go over your PIP limit, may be claimed against the at-fault driver; our page on <a href="/new-jersey/pip-coverage-limits-medical-bills-new-jersey#above-the-limit">medical bills over the PIP limit</a> explains how.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-7">7</a></sup></p>

</section>
<section id="pip-arbitration">
<h2>What is PIP arbitration, and how does it work step by step?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">It is the binding dispute program DOBI designates for PIP benefits, run by Forthright.</strong> Our sources do not settle whether you may sue in court instead under N.J.S.A. 39:6A-5.1.</p>
<ol class="df-steps">
<li><strong>Internal appeal:</strong> ask your provider to file the insurer's internal appeal; a provider filing as your assignee must finish it before arbitration.</li>
<li><strong>File with Forthright:</strong> a dispute resolution professional (DRP) decides the case in person, by phone or on the papers.</li>
<li><strong>Medical review:</strong> a Medical Review Organization report costs $1,450 for requests made on or after 15 May 2025.</li>
<li><strong>Appeal panel, then court:</strong> in Relievus (2018), the 45-day clock for a court challenge started at the DRP appeal panel's award.</li>
</ol>
<p>In Monmouth Medical Center (2019), the trial court vacated awards of $31,426, $21,403, $18,663 and $15,461, and a statute barred any further appeal.</p>
<p>In Hereford (2018), arbitration between insurers needed no in-person hearing. In Carteret (2025), an insurer's fraud and RICO claims against a provider went back to court, with a jury.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-8">8</a></sup></p>

<p>Other routes for a denied claim, such as the DOBI Ombudsman or a suit, are on our page about a <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-insurance-claim-denied">denied NJ car insurance claim</a>.</p>
</section>
<section id="faq" class="df-faqs">
<h2>Questions people ask</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>Does PIP coverage have to be paid back?</h3></summary>
<p>The one published ruling in our file on PIP recovery is between insurers. In Palisades (2021), a PIP carrier that paid bills the health plan owed first could not recover from the health plan, so it had to pursue the providers or use an assignment.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Do you have to pay a deductible for PIP?</h3></summary>
<p>Under health primary, your health plan's deductible and co-pays apply first. Under PIP primary, your auto policy's PIP deductible and co-payment apply. If your named plan had ended by the crash date, you owe an extra $750 deductible.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What is PIP in medical billing?</h3></summary>
<p>It is the auto claim a provider bills for car accident treatment. The provider must know which card is primary, so the bill goes to PIP first unless you chose health primary.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What happens if you don't have PIP insurance?</h3></summary>
<p>If no one in your household has auto insurance, you may be able to seek PIP through the Unsatisfied Claim and Judgment Fund, which NJ PLIGA runs. If you were driving your own uninsured car, see <a href="/new-jersey/uninsured-driver-hurt-in-nj-crash-can-you-sue">suing after a crash while uninsured</a>.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-9">9</a></sup></p>

</details></section>

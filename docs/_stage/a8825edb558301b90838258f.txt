<section id="deaths">
<h2>Is I-80 the deadliest road through Wayne?</h2>
<p>Yes: 4 of Wayne's 10 traffic deaths in 2023 and 2024 were on I-80, a road with only 63 of the town's 848 injury accidents.</p>

<h2>What else do people ask about a Wayne accident report?</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>Are accident reports public record in NJ?</h3></summary>
<p>The basic crash report is. Serious and fatal investigation reports are not, per the State Police portal. Our <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-accident-report">New Jersey accident report guide</a> covers who may request one.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Can I file an accident report online in NJ?</h3></summary>
<p>Yes, when no officer came: the SR-1 self-report is filed online with NJDOT. A knowing failure to make a required report carries a $30 to $100 fine.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>How to get a police report from the NJ State Police?</h3></summary>
<p>For an I-80 crash in Wayne, order it on the NJSP crash report portal with the $13 non-toll option. The report guide above has the records desk details.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Who is most at fault in a rear-end collision?</h3></summary>
<p>Not always the driver behind: New Jersey splits fault by percentage. Rear-end crashes are 36.9 percent of Wayne's injury accidents; our <a href="/new-jersey/rear-end-crash-new-jersey">rear-end crash guide</a> shows real fault splits.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>Do I need to call the cops if I get rear-ended?</h3></summary>
<p>If anyone is hurt, call 911. For anything else, Wayne Police's non-emergency line is 973-694-0600.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-7">7</a></sup></p>

<section id="deaths">
<h2>Who was killed in Freehold Township crashes?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">Seven people died in 2023 and 2024, and five of those deaths were on Route 9.</strong> Four of the five were pedestrians, all struck in the dark or at dawn.</p>
<p>Off Route 9, one person died in a left-turn crash on Wemrock Road and one in a head-on crash on Jackson Mills Road. Preliminary 2025 adds one more death on Jackson Mills Road, a fixed-object crash.</p>
<p>Pedestrian crashes are few but deadly here: 15 in 2023 and 2024, with 4 pedestrians killed. Our <a href="/new-jersey/pedestrian-crash-new-jersey">pedestrian crash guide</a> covers what PIP pays when you are hit on foot.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup></p>
</section>
<section id="when">
<h2>When do Freehold Township accidents happen?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">On weekdays, mostly from noon to 6 pm.</strong> Monday to Friday holds 404 of the 510 injury accidents, 79 percent, against about 75 percent statewide.</p>
<p>Noon to 3 pm carries 21.4 percent of injury accidents, above the statewide 18.1 percent, and 3 pm to 6 pm another 23.1 percent. Friday is the worst day with 97 and Sunday the quietest with 41.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup></p>
<p>The statewide hour-by-hour picture is on <a href="/new-jersey/when-do-most-car-accidents-happen">when car accidents happen in New Jersey</a>.</p>
</section>
<section id="report">
<h2>Who has your Freehold Township crash report?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">Almost always the township's own police.</strong> Only 16 of 1,901 Freehold Township records in 2024 and 2025 name a State Police station, all of them Hamilton, and 11 of those were on Route 537.</p>
<p>Freehold Borough is a separate municipality in the NJDOT records, so a crash on the Borough side of the line is not counted here. Check the town printed on your exchange slip before you ask for a report.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-7">7</a></sup></p>
<p>How to request a report, the State Police portal and fees are in <a href="/new-jersey/nj-car-accident-report">how to get your New Jersey accident report</a>. Next door, <a href="/new-jersey/wall-township-monmouth-nj-car-accidents">Wall Township crash data</a> shows a town where the Parkway sends many reports to the State Police. South on Route 9, see <a href="/new-jersey/lakewood-nj-car-accidents">Lakewood crash records</a>, or start from the <a href="/new-jersey/new-jersey-car-accident-help">New Jersey car accident help guide</a>.</p>
<p>To the southwest, <a href="/new-jersey/jackson-township-nj-car-accidents">car accidents in Jackson Township</a> happen mostly on county roads.</p>

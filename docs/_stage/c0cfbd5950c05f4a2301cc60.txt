<h1>Route 36 NJ accident today: rear end crashes hurt the most, pedestrian and bike crashes kill</h1>

  <p class="lead">Route 36's police records show a split. Rear end collisions are the most common kind of Route 36 accident where someone is hurt, yet none of them killed anyone. <section id="report">
  <h2>Town police wrote almost every Route 36 report</h2>
  <p>On Route 36, the report almost always comes from the town police department. Only 2 of the 1,860 Route 36 records from 2023 to 2025 (2025 preliminary) name a State Police station. Your request goes to the department of the town where the crash happened, and the milepost table above shows the order of the towns along the road.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Middletown:</strong> the Middletown Township Police Department page links "Accident Reports" to the CrashDocs online report service. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.middletownnj.org/224/Police-Department">Middletown Township Police Department</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></li>
    <li><strong>Eatontown:</strong> the Eatontown Police Department page lists the department at 47 Broad Street, Eatontown, phone (732) 542-0100. We did not find an online report request there. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.eatontownnj.com/departments/police-department">Borough of Eatontown, Police Department</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></li>
    <li><strong>If the State Police responded:</strong> request the report through the official NJSP crash report portal, which lists NJSPRecords@NJSP.gov and (609) 882-2000 ext. 2234 for questions. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.njportal.com/njsp/crashreports/">NJSP crash reports on NJ Portal</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span></li>
  </ul>
  <p>The police report form, the NJTR-1, is filled out by police only. If no officer investigated, drivers report the accident themselves on the SR-1 form. <span class="source">(<a href="https://dot.nj.gov/transportation/refdata/accident/policeres.shtm">NJDOT Police Resources</a>, retrieved 2026-11-10)</span> Request steps and fees for other towns vary by department and are not verified here, so call the department's records unit.</p>

<section id="coverage">
  <h2>Short 2023 counts in West Long Branch, Long Branch and Eatontown</h2>
  <p>NJDOT's 2023 and 2024 records are treated as complete in our data. For Route 36, 2023 still looks short in some towns. West Long Branch has no Route 36 accidents in 2023 against 62 in 2024. Long Branch has 34 against 87, and Eatontown 91 against 191. The same drop shows on all roads in those towns, so it looks like a recording gap, not safer driving.</p>
  <p>That is why Route 36's 2023 total (500) sits so far below 2024 (690). The two-year figures in this page, 1,190 accidents and 309 with someone hurt or killed, are therefore likely an undercount.</p>
  <p>For 2025, the route-wide total is 97 percent of 2024, which looks nearly complete. Town by town it is not even. Hazlet's 2025 Route 36 total is 58 percent of its 2024 total, Keyport's 67 percent and Keansburg has a single record. Lower 2025 numbers in those towns should not be read as a drop in accidents.</p>

<section class="place-common" id="what-these-numbers-mean">
  <h2>What these numbers can and cannot tell you</h2>
  <p>The counts on this page are police-reported car accidents from NJDOT crash records, analyzed by us. They show where and when accidents where someone was hurt happened on Route 36. They do not show who was at fault in any accident, and they say nothing about whether you have a claim or what it could be worth.</p>
  <h3>Rules that apply anywhere in New Jersey</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Your own auto policy's PIP pays your medical bills "no matter who caused the auto accident." A claim against the other driver for pain and suffering is separate and depends on fault and on the lawsuit option on your policy. <span class="source">(<a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/division_consumers/pdf/autoguide2026.pdf">NJ DOBI Auto Insurance Buyer's Guide 2026</a>, p. 2)</span></li>
    <li>Being partly at fault does not stop you from recovering, as long as your share is not greater than the other driver's (the "51 percent" rule). Your damages are reduced by your share. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:15-5.1; <a href="https://www.nj.gov/dobi/ins_ombudsman/ombuds_acnsfaq.html">NJ DOBI comparative negligence FAQ</a>)</span></li>
    <li>If someone is hurt, the driver must tell the police right away. If police did not investigate, New Jersey's SR-1 self-reporting form is the form for that. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 39:4-130; <a href="https://dot.nj.gov/transportation/refdata/accident/selfreporting.shtm">NJDOT self-reporting crash form</a>)</span></li>
    <li>An injury lawsuit generally must be filed within two years, and a claim against a public body (a town, the state, NJ Transit) needs a written notice within 90 days. These are general rules with exceptions; check your own dates with a lawyer. <span class="source">(N.J.S.A. 2A:14-2; N.J.S.A. 59:8-8)</span></li>
  </ul>

<section id="method">
  <h2>How we counted Route 36</h2>
  <p>We counted records where the route number is 36 and the road name starts with a state prefix, which includes "NJ 36", "NJ 36 SECONDARY" and the named forms such as "NJ 36 (JOLINE AVENUE)". Records filed under the Monmouth County road numbered 36 in Middletown are not included. An accident counts as "hurt or killed" when the police report lists at least one person injured or killed.</p>
  <p>The rule has small edges in the 2025 preliminary data. Two West Long Branch records written as "NJ 71 (MONMOUTH ROAD)" and "NJ 71 SB" carry route number 36 and are counted. Five records written without the state prefix ("HIGHWAY 36", "SR 36", "STATE ROUTE 36" and two "JOLINE AVENUE") are left out.</p>
  <p>Headline two-year counts use 2023 and 2024. Town figures use 2024, and cross streets use 2024 and 2025, since NJDOT's 2023 export spells Route 36 cross streets differently from the 2024 and 2025 files. 2025 is preliminary and will grow as late reports arrive. Every figure is a count of police reports, not a risk rate, since there is no traffic volume behind it.</p>

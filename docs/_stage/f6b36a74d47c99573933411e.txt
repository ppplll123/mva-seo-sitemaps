<p><strong>What to do:</strong> tell your doctor about it in those words, so it goes in your notes next to the physical injuries.</p>

<section id="how-long">
<h2>How long does it last?</h2>
<p>Often longer than people around you expect. 148 posts said how long ago the crash was, and about half of them were written 12 months or more after it.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>32 posts were written in the first month. 22 were written three years or more after the crash, and a few came decades later. Struggling a year on does not mean something is wrong with you.</p>
<p>If you were hurt in New Jersey, the time matters for another reason: you usually have two years from the crash to sue the driver who caused it.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup></p>
</section>
<section id="thoughts-of-dying">
<h2>What if you have wished you had not survived?</h2>
<p>You are not the only one, and you can say it out loud. 8 of the 387 posts said the writer had wished they had died, or had thought about ending their life.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup></p>
<p>If that is you right now, call, text or chat with 988 at any hour: in New Jersey it is confidential, and you can talk in English or Spanish.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> If you are in danger, call 911.</p>
<p>For support that is not a crisis, NJ MentalHealthCares answers at 1-866-202-HELP (4357), 8 am to 8 pm every day.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup></p>

<section id="who-pays-nj">
<h2>Who pays for help after a car accident in New Jersey?</h2>
<p><strong class="df-k">Your own car insurance pays first, no matter who caused the crash.</strong> That medical part (PIP) covers treatment from licensed providers, and the law names licensed psychologists among them.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup></p>
<p>If you can't work, New Jersey's temporary disability pays first if you qualify, and the lost-pay part of your car insurance, if you have it, can add to it.<sup class="df-fn"><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup></p>
<ul>
<li>Counseling and therapy bills: <a href="/new-jersey/who-pays-for-therapy-after-a-car-accident-nj">who pays for therapy after a car accident in NJ</a>.</li>
<li>Fear of driving and flashbacks: <a href="/new-jersey/ptsd-driving-anxiety-after-car-accident-nj">scared to drive after a crash</a>.</li>
<li>If you caused it: <a href="/new-jersey/guilt-after-a-car-accident-i-caused">guilt after a crash you caused</a>.</li>
</ul>

<section id="faq" class="df-faqs">
<h2>Questions people ask</h2>
<details class="df-faq" open><summary><h3>How does a car accident affect you emotionally?</h3></summary>
<p>In more ways than people expect. Besides fear and panic, people wrote about feeling alone (36 posts), not being believed (25) and being worn out (25), often several at once.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>How long does it take to emotionally recover from a car accident?</h3></summary>
<p>There is no set time, and you are not behind if you still struggle. If you are still hurting a year later, you are not alone: about half of the posts that said when the crash was came a year or more after it.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>How do you cope after a car accident that was your fault?</h3></summary>
<p>Guilt is common, and it showed up in 9 posts by drivers who caused their own crash. Our page on <a href="/new-jersey/guilt-after-a-car-accident-i-caused">guilt after a crash you caused</a> has steps that helped.</p>
</details><details class="df-faq"><summary><h3>What are the signs of emotional trauma after an accident?</h3></summary>
<p>The ones people wrote about most were panic (94 posts), being scared to drive (57), low mood (53), anger (48) and replaying the crash (42). If that sounds like you, tell a doctor, so it is treated and written down.</p>
</details></section>

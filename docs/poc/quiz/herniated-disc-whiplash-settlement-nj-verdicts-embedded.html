How much should I settle for a herniated disc?

Our 9 disc claims split three ways: 2 met the threshold, 4 did not, and 3 were sent back. Each one is below.1

What is the hardest injury to prove?

In our New Jersey record, a disc herniation: 4 of the 6 herniation claims that got a threshold ruling ended with no pain and suffering award.1

The four losses split three ways. Orlando lacked proof of permanence or cause, Williams-Siraj lacked proof the other drivers caused her injuries, and Joo lacked medical evidence the crash worsened an older condition. Zavis lost on a fresh review of the record.

The diagnosis label did not separate winners from losers. Radiculopathy, nerve pain spreading from the spine, appears in both wins and in two losses, Zavis and Williams-Siraj.

Words about an earlier, worsened condition appear in four claims: one won (Presbery), two lost and one went back for a new trial. What counted was medical proof of the change, not the phrase.

How much compensation can I expect for a bulging disc after a car accident?

Our record cannot price a bulge on its own. Bulges appear in 8 of the 9 disc claims, but every one of those also had a herniation.1

The only bulge case with a dollar figure is Morales-Hurtado, a Bergen jitney rear-end: $50,000 for pain and suffering plus $71,615.73 in past medical bills. The appeals court reversed that verdict, so it is not a benchmark either.

How much do whiplash cases usually settle for?

None of our coded injury descriptions uses the word whiplash, so our record gives no whiplash figure. Our coded descriptions list cervical strain or sprain instead.1

Three disc claims list a neck strain or sprain, with three different endings. Presbery kept $240,000, Joo got nothing for pain and suffering, and McKoy won a new trial.

In all three the strain sat beside disc herniations, so the strain alone cannot be priced from these cases. When whiplash pain tends to start is on how long car accident symptoms take to show up.

Does a herniated disc meet the verbal threshold in NJ?

It can, with a doctor's permanency certification served within 60 days after the defendant answers the lawsuit.2 The proof rules are on ways to prove pain and suffering in New Jersey, and the two lawsuit options on is NJ a no-fault state.

Read against the nine cases, a disc claim needs four things in the file before any number means much:

  1. The lawsuit option on your declarations page. With no limitation on lawsuit, the permanency test does not apply.
  2. A timely permanency certification resting on imaging or other objective findings.
  3. A medical opinion that this crash caused the disc injury, the gap in Orlando and Williams-Siraj.
  4. If your spine showed wear before, medical proof that the crash made it worse, the gap in Joo.

How much does accident insurance pay for a herniated disc?

Medical bills go first to your own PIP, and DOBI says most drivers choose a $250,000 medical limit.3 How that works is on PIP coverage limits for medical bills.

Zavis and Joo claimed against their own insurers under UM or UIM coverage, and both still failed on permanency. Those disputes are on NJ uninsured and underinsured motorist appeals, and more statewide guides on the New Jersey car accident help hub.

Questions people ask

What is the average settlement for a herniated disc in a car accident?

No source we hold gives one for New Jersey, and settled claims never reach the appeals court. In our record 4 of 6 herniation threshold rulings ended at zero for pain and suffering, so an average would describe no real claimant.

Do steroid injections increase settlement?

Our record cannot say. None of the coded injury descriptions in the 76 decisions mentions an injection, so we have no case to compare.

How much is the average whiplash settlement?

No source we hold states one for New Jersey. The three disc claims coded with a neck strain ended at a $240,000 non-economic award, nothing for pain and suffering, and a new trial.
